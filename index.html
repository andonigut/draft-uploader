Draft Uploader

Draft Uploader is a small publishing tool used by our studio to send finished short vertical videos to the TikTok accounts we manage, as drafts in the TikTok inbox. Every video is reviewed, captioned and published by the account owner inside the TikTok app.

It uses TikTok Login Kit to let each account owner authorize the upload, and the Content Posting API (upload to inbox) to deliver the video file. Nothing is posted automatically.

Privacy Policy

Draft Uploader only requests the TikTok permissions needed to upload a video to the authorizing user's TikTok inbox (user.info.basic and video.upload).

We store the access and refresh tokens and the account's open_id locally on our own computer, only to upload videos to that same account. We do not collect, sell or share any other personal data, and we do not read the user's videos, followers or messages.

The account owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions). Tokens are deleted when access is revoked.
